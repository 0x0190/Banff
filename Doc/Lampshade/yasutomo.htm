Paper Lampshade
Written by Karen Elaine   
Wednesday, 15 July 2009 12:21

Fold up a beautiful paper lampshade and cast a soft glow of light to enhance any room. In this project a light fixture with an outdated lampshade was used as the base. The paper shade can also be set over a glass candle for a beautiful luminaria. Fold several paper shades in different sizes for a dramatic effect.

 

 

  • Supplies
  • Instructions
  • Tips

Supplies

You will need:
•    1 piece medium to heavyweight paper 14" x 22"
•    Double sided tape
•    Bone Folder
•    Fire retardant spray
•    Lamp or glass candle

Instructions



Treat the paper with fire retardant before folding. There are several fire retardant sprays available. The one used in this project is from Arnold Grummer. Fold the paper according to the diagram below. Make sure your creases are very sharp!

Tips

Use strong fibrous papers such as masa paper or medium weight rice papers.
